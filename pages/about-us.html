ABOUT FACULTYMS

Online Faculty & Staff Directory System

An efficient, centralized web portal designed to streamline access to university faculty information. FacultyMS allows students, researchers, and visitors to quickly locate professor contact details, academic departments, courses taught, and professional areas of expertise—all without registration.

👨‍🏫

Faculty Directory

View comprehensive professor profiles with high-resolution photos, direct email links, courses taught, and research tags.

🔍

Multi-Filter Search

Instantly refine directory searches across 5 distinct criteria: Professor Name, Department, Courses Taught, Expertise, and Interests.

⚡

Admin Management

Authorized personnel have full administrative controls to register new faculty, update academic records, and manage departments.

📊

Real-Time Analytics

Live metrics and visual department breakdown graphics providing instant insights into university staff allocation.
