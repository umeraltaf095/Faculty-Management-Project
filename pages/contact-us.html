Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need an account to search for faculty? ▼
No, guest visitors can search and view all faculty profiles, courses taught, and areas of expertise without registration.
How do admins add or update a faculty profile? ▼
Authorized admins log in with admin credentials, navigate to the Faculty Directory or Main Dashboard, and click "Add Faculty" or the edit icon on any profile card.
Which search filters are available? ▼
You can refine searches across 5 criteria: Name, Department, Courses Taught, Area of Expertise, and Professional Interests.
